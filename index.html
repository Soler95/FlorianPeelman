Biography

About
Florian Peelman

Florian Peelman is a violist known for his warm, expressive sound and his ability to bring depth and subtle nuance to both solo and chamber music repertoire.

As a teacher, he prepares students for highly competitive orchestral auditions, and his former students now hold positions in leading professional orchestras around the world.
